TCC PENS is the cybersecurity community of the Internet Engineering Technology program at PENS. Members train together, compete in CTFs, and conduct research.

## Community and research

Students explore how security issues arise in applications and infrastructure, how to interpret digital evidence, and how to assess security controls. Our areas of study include web security, cryptography, reverse engineering, digital forensics and incident response, and security governance.

The member directory introduces the people working in each area. The portfolio documents the community’s research, training, and competition work. [Meet the members](https://tccpens.id/members/) · [Explore the portfolio](https://tccpens.id/portfolio/)
